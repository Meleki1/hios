# HIOS

Home Intelligence Operating System

HIOS is an event-driven intelligence platform designed to transform
real-world observations into trustworthy decisions.

HIOS is not a chatbot.

HIOS is not an AI framework.

HIOS is the intelligence layer powering home-aware applications.

---

## Philosophy

Reality
↓

Observation
↓

Understanding
↓

Decision
↓

Execution
↓

Outcome
↓

Learning

---

## Principles

- Reality is the source of truth.
- Facts and decisions are separated.
- Intelligence is modular.
- Every decision is explainable.
- Every completed case improves future decisions.
